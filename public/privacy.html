Privacy policy

Atmosphere has no accounts, advertising SDK, payment processor, or project-operated user database. Optional analytics stays off until you allow it.

Effective 18 September 2026

Information stored on your device

Browser or WebView storage keeps settings, analytics consent, saved and recent mixes, favorite atmospheres, playback state, and resume information. It is not synchronized to Atmosphere servers. Clearing site or application data removes it.

Shared mixes are encoded in the generated link. Anyone who receives the link can read and load its mix configuration.

Optional analytics

After you grant consent, Google Analytics 4 receives page views, selected atmosphere and track identifiers, playback and feature interactions, listening and active-time milestones, coarse viewport categories, performance measurements, and an approximate region derived by Google from the network connection. Google can also process technical information such as IP address, browser, operating system, referring page, and timestamps.

Atmosphere disables Google Signals, advertising storage, ad personalization, and ad-user-data consent. Precise GPS coordinates are never added to analytics events. Event-level data may be retained for up to 14 months; aggregated reports can remain longer.

Change your choice any timeOpen Settings → Privacy. Withdrawing consent stops future collection, although it cannot remove information already included in aggregated reports.

Optional local weather

Weather remains off until you select Add local weather and grant browser or operating-system permission. Coordinates are rounded to two decimal places—roughly one kilometre depending on location—and sent directly to Open-Meteo. Atmosphere keeps them only in memory, never attaches them to analytics, and has no location database. Open-Meteo applies its own logging and privacy practices.

Hosting, providers, and international processing

GitHub hosts the website, source, releases, installation files, and primary media. Google provides consented analytics. Open-Meteo provides requested weather. Credited media hosts can serve some video. Those providers may process network and request data in other countries under their own terms and safeguards.

Atmosphere does not sell personal information or share it for cross-context behavioral advertising.

Purposes and legal bases

Local data provides requested playback and personalization. Analytics relies on consent and helps measure reliability, feature use, and whether future funding would be appropriate. Location is used only to fulfill the requested weather feature. Hosting providers can retain limited security logs to operate their services safely.

Your choices and rights

You can deny or withdraw analytics, decline location access, clear application data, remove saved mixes, and avoid sharing links. Depending on where you live, you may also have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to processing and to complain to a data-protection authority. Because Atmosphere has no accounts and intentionally collects no direct identifier, maintainers may be unable to associate an analytics record with a specific person.

Children

Atmosphere is a general-audience tool and is not directed to children under 13. A child who cannot legally consent to analytics in their location should keep analytics disabled unless a parent or guardian provides required permission.

Contact and changes

For a privacy request, open an issue without posting sensitive information and ask for a private channel. Material changes update the effective date and published application links.
