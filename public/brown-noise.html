Questions

About this soundscape

What is brown noise?

Brown noise emphasizes lower frequencies more than white noise, producing a deeper and softer rumble.

Can I play brown noise without video?

Yes. Audio-only mode removes video loading and is useful for sleep, background listening and data saving.

Is brown noise a medical treatment?

No. Atmosphere is a relaxation and focus tool, not medical care. Keep the volume comfortable and seek professional advice for hearing or sleep concerns.
