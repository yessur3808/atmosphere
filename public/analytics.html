Optional measurement

Analytics disclosure

Atmosphere measures product use only after affirmative consent, without accounts, advertising identifiers, precise location, or user-entered text.

Effective 27 September 2026

Consent and provider

The Google Analytics 4 tag is not requested until you select Allow analytics. Rejection keeps the tag unloaded. Consent can be changed under Settings → Privacy & cookies. Google acts as the analytics provider and may process technical request information under its own terms.

What is measured

  • Page views, referrer, browser, operating system, coarse device and viewport categories, and approximate region inferred by Google from the connection.
  • Atmosphere, sound-layer, video-loop, recipe, mix, search/filter, settings, quiet-view, and mini-player interactions.
  • Playback starts and pauses, master and layer-volume commits, listening intervals and milestones, and session-level active/listening/quiet time.
  • Media quality choice, delivered audio/video quality, browser connection category, Save-Data hint, Data Saver choice, media errors, and Core Web Vitals.
  • Weather feature state and coarse condition only. Coordinates, place names, and city-search text are excluded.

What is deliberately excluded

Atmosphere does not send names, email addresses, account identifiers, custom mix names, shared-mix contents, full media URLs, GPS coordinates, city-search text, or advertising identifiers. Advertising storage, ad-user-data consent, ad personalization, and Google Signals remain denied.

Purpose and retention

Measurement is used to understand reliability, feature usefulness, popular atmospheres and sounds, media delivery quality, accessibility across device classes, and whether future non-intrusive funding could be viable. GA cookies are configured for approximately thirteen months. Event-level retention is configured for up to fourteen months; aggregate reporting can remain longer.

Control and withdrawal

Analytics is optionalReject it in the initial prompt or under Settings → Privacy & cookies. Withdrawal stops future Atmosphere events, sends denied consent, activates the GA disable flag, and attempts to remove accessible analytics cookies. Browser site-data controls can remove remaining data on the device.

Global Privacy Control or Do Not Track defaults analytics to denied when no earlier choice exists. Withdrawal cannot erase data already processed or aggregated by Google.

Implementation documentation

The public source repository includes the event inventory and measurement plan. See the privacy policy and cookie policy for the broader data-handling context.
