Questions

About this soundscape

Is Traffic part of City Sounds?

Yes. Traffic is an explicit subcategory within the broader City Sounds atmosphere.

Can I combine traffic with rain?

Yes. Wet-street and rainy-commute combinations work well as starting points, and every layer remains adjustable.

Does the city atmosphere contain music?

The standard city layers use environmental sound. Music is clearly separated into atmospheres such as Elevator Music.
