About this soundscape

Are these actual nature recordings?

Atmosphere uses credited field recordings and clearly labels instrumental tracks where music is intentionally included.

Can I combine forest and waterfall sounds?

Yes. The Forest Stream recipe is a quick starting point, and individual layers can be changed afterward.

Where can I check the sources?

Audio and video credit pages list the source, creator, license and modifications for active media.
