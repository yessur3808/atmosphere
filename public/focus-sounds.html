About this soundscape

Can I combine office and nature sounds?

Yes. Curated recipes combine compatible layers, and the mixer lets you create and save your own balance.

Can I share a focus mix?

Yes. Shared mix links contain only the playback configuration, not your custom mix name or personal details.

Will Atmosphere block distractions?

Atmosphere supplies a consistent background but does not block notifications or websites. Pair it with your device's focus controls when useful.
