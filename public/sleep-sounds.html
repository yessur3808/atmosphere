Questions

About this soundscape

Which atmosphere is best for sleep?

Rainy Bedroom, Brown Noise, Ocean Waves and Wind are useful starting points. The best choice is the one that feels steady and unobtrusive to you.

Will the video pause with the audio?

Linked playback pauses both audio and background motion together. Separate controls remain available as an optional preference.

Does Atmosphere require an account?

No. Favorites, mixes and recent playback are stored locally on your device.
