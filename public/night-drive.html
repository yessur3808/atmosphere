Questions

About this soundscape

Are the night-drive videos different routes?

Yes. The library contains distinct city, freeway, tunnel, coast, winter and rural footage rather than visual effects applied to one source.

Can I pause the road and sound together?

Yes. Linked playback makes the main button control both. A preference can restore separate controls.

Can I use Night Drive in Audio only?

Yes. The road ambience continues while video requests are disabled.
