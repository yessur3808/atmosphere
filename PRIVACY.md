# Privacy policy

Effective: 18 September 2026

This policy explains how the Atmosphere project ("Atmosphere", "we", or "us") handles information in the web and desktop applications.

## Summary

Atmosphere has no account system, advertising SDK, payment processor, or project-operated user database. Preferences and mixes stay on the device. Google Analytics loads only after the user grants analytics consent. Precise location is never sent to Google Analytics.

## Information handled

### Data stored on the device

Atmosphere uses browser or WebView storage for settings, analytics consent, saved and recent mixes, favorite atmospheres, playback state, and resume information. This data is not synchronized to Atmosphere servers. Clearing site/application data removes it.

Shared mixes are encoded in the link generated by the app. Anyone who receives that link can read and load the mix configuration.

### Optional analytics

If analytics consent is granted, Atmosphere loads Google Analytics 4 and sends page views, selected atmosphere and track identifiers, playback and feature interactions, listening and active-time milestones, coarse viewport/device categories, performance measurements, and an approximate region derived by Google from the network connection. Atmosphere disables Google Signals, advertising storage, ad personalization, and ad-user-data consent.

Google may process technical information such as IP address, browser, device, operating system, referring page, and timestamps to provide the service. Atmosphere does not place precise GPS coordinates in analytics events. GA4 event-level data may be retained for up to 14 months; aggregated reports can remain longer. Consent can be withdrawn at any time in **Settings → Privacy**. Withdrawal stops future collection but does not retroactively delete aggregated reports.

### Optional local weather

Weather is off until the user selects **Add local weather** and grants the operating system or browser permission. Coordinates are rounded to two decimal places (roughly one kilometre, depending on location) and sent directly to Open-Meteo to retrieve current conditions. Atmosphere keeps coordinates only in memory, does not attach them to analytics, and does not store them in its own database. Open-Meteo processes requests under its own privacy policy and may retain service logs.

### Hosting and downloads

GitHub hosts the website, repository, releases, and installation files. GitHub can process network and request logs under its own privacy statement. Audio and video may also be loaded from the public GitHub Pages origin or a credited media provider.

## Purposes and legal bases

Local data is used to provide requested playback and personalization features. Optional analytics is processed only after consent to understand reliability, feature use, and whether future funding or monetization would be appropriate. Weather location is processed only at the user's request to provide local conditions. Where applicable law recognizes legitimate interests, basic security and abuse-prevention logs may be processed to operate the hosting service safely.

## Sharing and international processing

Information is shared only with providers needed for the requested feature: Google Analytics after consent, Open-Meteo after the weather request, GitHub for hosting and releases, and the media hosts named in the credits. These providers can process data in countries other than the user's own and apply their own safeguards and retention rules. Atmosphere does not sell personal information or share it for cross-context behavioral advertising.

## Choices and rights

Users can deny or withdraw analytics consent, decline location permission, clear saved application data, remove individual saved mixes, and avoid sharing mix links. Depending on location, users may also have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to processing and to complain to a data-protection authority. Because Atmosphere has no accounts and intentionally receives no direct identifier, the maintainers may be unable to connect an analytics record to a particular person.

For a privacy request, open an issue at <https://github.com/yessur3808/atmosphere/issues> without posting sensitive information and ask the maintainers for a private contact channel.

## Children

Atmosphere is a general-audience relaxation tool and is not directed to children under 13. A child who cannot legally consent to analytics in their location should use the app with analytics disabled unless a parent or guardian provides any required permission.

## Security and changes

Reasonable technical and organizational safeguards are used, but no networked service is risk-free. Security reports should follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Material policy changes will update the effective date and the published application links.
