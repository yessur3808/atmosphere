# Accessibility statement

Effective: 18 September 2026

Atmosphere aims to provide a usable experience with keyboard navigation, semantic controls, visible focus states, descriptive labels, scalable layouts, reduced-motion support, and text alternatives for meaningful controls.

Known limitations can include reduced contrast caused by some video backgrounds, WebView or browser differences in media and picture-in-picture controls, and media that is experiential rather than transcript-based. The interface adds dark overlays and glass surfaces to preserve readability, and users can choose quiet view or audio-only mode.

To report an accessibility problem, open an issue at <https://github.com/yessur3808/atmosphere/issues> and include the page or control, operating system, browser or app version, assistive technology, and expected behavior. Do not include sensitive personal information.
